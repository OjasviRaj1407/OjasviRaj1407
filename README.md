### 03. 🏸 ShuttleNetra : AI-Powered Badminton Analytics
**Computer Vision & Player Action Recognition**

A high-throughput computer vision pipeline engineered to analyze badminton gameplay, track high-velocity shuttlecock trajectories, and classify player body mechanics.

```text
Match Broadcast / Video
          ↓
  Frame Preprocessing
          ↓
  Player Detection (YOLOv8)
          ↓
 Pose & Stroke Classification
          ↓
 Shuttlecock Trajectory Tracking
          ↓
  Court Spatial Analytics
          ↓
 Performance Diagnostic Report
```

* **Tech Stack:** `Python` • `PyTorch` • `YOLOv8` • `OpenCV` • `ONNX Runtime` • `Flask` • `Roboflow`
* **Dataset & Training:** 900+ annotated shuttlecock frames, 1,200+ pose classification instances with auto-orientation and augmentation pipelines.

# 💭 Engineering Mindset

> **“Find the true signal.”**  
> **“Question the implicit assumptions.”**  
> **“Keep the system as simple as the problem allows.”**  
> **“Engineer for tangible impact, not synthetic complexity.”**

* **Pareto's Principle (80/20):** Focus relentlessly on the core 20% of mechanisms that drive 80% of systemic reliability and performance.
* **Occam’s Razor:** Never introduce distributed overhead or complex model architectures when an elegant, deterministic solution suffices.
